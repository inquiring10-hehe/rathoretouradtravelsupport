About us

Go where you feel most alive.

Rathore Tour & Travels is a govt. registered travel company, established in 2012 and proudly owned by ex-army man Tej Singh Rathore and managed by Rahul Rathore. Discipline, punctuality and honesty are the way we work.

We are Uttarakhand focused, so we know the hills, the roads and the weather like home. From Char Dham yatra to Nainital, Rishikesh and beyond, we plan every trip with care. We also run cabs, tours and holidays across India.
